1980s Era

The Spark of Curiosity & Electronics Mastery

Fascinated by circuit mechanics behind festival pandals (Flasher units). Self-taught fundamentals of electricity under parental mentorship. Built an LED Multiplication Table project, winning 1st Place at Abanpola Central College Science Exhibition & earned the title "Electronic Ravi".

1990s Era

Programming & Entry to Public Service

Advanced into software development via GW BASIC at St. Anne's College. Awarded full scholarship for Software Engineering Diploma at TEC Sri Lanka. Entered Public Service in late 90s as Data Entry Operator at NW Chief Ministry with top exam score, balancing service with music band leadership and audio engineering.

2000s Era

Systems Innovation & Public Sector Software

Developed and donated 'TPSoft' standalone telephone directory software. Built critical software solutions for Early Childhood Development Authority & Community Water Supply Project (evaluated by US delegation). Promoted to Computer Program Analyst at Chief Secretariat. Initiated province-wide Hardware & Networking training workshops for officers.

2010s Era

Pioneering Web, Fiber & International Exposure

Secured first ADSL line in NW Province & launched official provincial portal (csnwp.lk). Volunteer System Admin for Lanka Government Network (LGN 1.0). Led FTTH Migration Feasibility Project in 2014/15. Completed advanced training at MTNL Mumbai, India (Next-Gen Networks/5G) and Shenzhen, China (Ubuntu Keyling OS).

2020s Present

Digital Unit Leadership, Cloud & AI Automation

Established and leading the official Digital Division & GIS Unit (10 Officers). Deployed Centralized Containerized Cloud Infrastructure (Red Hat OpenShift, MySQL, PHP/Bootstrap). Coaching staff on AI Tools for software engineering and automated workflow generation.
